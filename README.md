A one-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance: the shower curtain that will not stop clinging to you. This one
solves it by behaving like a startled cat.

## The pitch

> A shower curtain that, the moment it tries to cling to your wet skin, recoils like a startled cat, hisses softly, and retreats to its rail until you have finished.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single sentence — deterministically, with no network
access and no randomness — to `/output/entry.txt`.
