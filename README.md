A one-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance: the shower curtain that will not stop clinging to you. This one
solves it by inflating itself into a rigid little tent, so it physically
cannot touch you — and it stays inflated until the water stops.

## The pitch

> A shower curtain that, the moment it clings to your wet skin, puffs itself into a rigid little tent with a built-in fan and stays inflated until the water stops.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single sentence — deterministically, with no network
access and no randomness — to `/output/entry.txt`.
